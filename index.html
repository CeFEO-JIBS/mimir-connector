What it does

Not a search box. You ask the question you would ask a colleague.

Answers, not results

Ask about a founder who won't step back, siblings deadlocked over a sale, a passive board. Mímir searches the whole library and answers with what the field actually knows.

Always sourced

Every claim points back to the article, summary or teaching unit it came from, so you can read the original and judge it yourself.

Connected across sources

A practitioner article, the research behind it and the module that teaches it are linked — so one question surfaces all three.
